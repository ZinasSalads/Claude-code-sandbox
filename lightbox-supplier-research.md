## Common Features Across Suppliers

- **SEG (Silicone Edge Graphics):** Fabric prints with a silicone strip sewn into the edge that slots into an aluminum frame for a seamless, frameless look
- **LED Backlighting:** Integrated LED strips provide even, bright illumination
- **Tool-free Assembly:** Most systems use snap-together or bungee-connected aluminum frames
- **Portable:** Designed to pack into travel cases for repeated show use
- **Custom Graphics:** Dye-sublimation printed fabric that is replaceable and often machine-washable
- **Standard Sizes:** Most common are 8ft, 10ft, and 20ft widths at approximately 8ft height
